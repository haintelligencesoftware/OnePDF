Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

OnePDF ("we", "the app") is built so your documents stay with you. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

Short version

  • Your documents are processed on your device. We do not upload, view or store them.
  • We have no accounts, no sign-in and no servers that hold your data.
  • The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising data — and only tracks you across apps if you allow it.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, documents, scans, recordings, signatures or the content of anything you open in the app. We run no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.

Documents and files

PDFs and files you import, create, scan or record are stored on your device in the app's storage. If you enable iCloud for the app, they are stored in your iCloud Drive and handled by Apple under its own privacy terms. The share extension and widget exchange only document names and identifiers with the app on your device (through an App Group).

On-device features

Text recognition (OCR), translation, summaries, chat, study tools and Document AI run on your device using Apple's frameworks (Vision, Translation and Apple Intelligence). Your document text is not sent to us or to any third-party AI service.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a rewarded ad before saving. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use device identifiers, advertising data and approximate usage information to deliver and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. On iOS the app asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any cross-app tracking; if you decline, ads are not personalized using tracking. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. OnePDF Premium removes the ad.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks with Apple whether a subscription is active.

Permissions

TrackingOptional; used only for ad personalization (see above).

Network use

The app connects to the internet to load ads (free version), to process purchases through Apple, and to check the App Store for newer versions of the app (Apple's public lookup service, which receives your IP address as any web request does). Nothing about your documents is included in these requests.

Children

OnePDF is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data held by Google (advertising) or Apple (iCloud, purchases), use those companies' privacy controls. If you have questions or requests under laws such as GDPR, KVKK or CCPA, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app or the App Store listing.
