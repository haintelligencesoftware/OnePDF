Frequently asked questions

How do I remove the ad before saving?

Subscribe to OnePDF Premium from the crown button on the Home screen. Premium removes the ad; every tool is free either way.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → OnePDF and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. We cannot cancel or refund purchases ourselves; refund requests go through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my files stored?

On your device in OnePDF's storage, or in your iCloud Drive if iCloud is enabled for the app. We never receive a copy of your documents.

Why are the AI features unavailable on my device?

Summaries, chat, study mode and Document AI use Apple Intelligence, which requires a supported device, iOS 26 or later, and Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Translation requires iOS 18 or later.

The scanned page isn't searchable.

Open Make Searchable on Home, choose the document and the language, and run OCR.

Can I edit existing text in a PDF?

Not yet. You can annotate, add text boxes, fill forms, redact, sign and reorganize pages. Editing existing text is not currently supported.

Are digital signatures legally binding?

OnePDF creates standard PKI signatures and can verify them. Legal recognition depends on your country and the type of document — check local requirements for contracts that need a qualified signature.

A file won't open or looks wrong.

Try opening it in another PDF app to check whether the file itself is damaged. If it opens elsewhere but not in OnePDF, email it to us only if it contains nothing sensitive, or describe how it was created.

Report a bug or request a feature

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL. Feature ideas are read and appreciated.
